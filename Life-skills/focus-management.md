# Focus Management

## Question 1: What is Deep Work?
- Deep Work is the ability to focus without distraction on cognitively demanding tasks.
- It helps produce high-quality and meaningful results in less time.

---

## Question 2: How to Do Deep Work Properly?
- Schedule deep work sessions in advance
- Remove all distractions before starting
- Work in focused time blocks
- Avoid multitasking
- Train your mind to focus regularly
- Treat deep work like an important commitment

---

## Question 3: How Can I Implement This in My Daily Life?
- Keep my phone on silent during work
- Block social media during study hours
- Plan 1–2 deep work sessions daily
- Work in 45–60 minute focus blocks
- Take short breaks between sessions
- Track my focused hours

---

## Question 4: Dangers of Social Media
- Reduces attention span
- Causes constant distraction
- Wastes productive time
- Increases comparison and anxiety
- Reduces deep thinking ability
- Creates addiction to quick rewards

---

## Intentionally Creating Diffused Mode
- Walk without phone
- Sit quietly for a few minutes
- Practice meditation
- Do physical chores mindfully
- Give brain time to relax

---

## Getting Started on Difficult Tasks
- Work for 30 seconds on the task
- Take a short break
- Remind yourself you are capable
- Repeat the process
- Gradually increase focus time
- Build small proof of progress
